Understanding PID Control

The PID controller calculates an error value as the difference between a desired setpoint and a measured process variable and applies a correction.

P - Proportional

"The Present". Reacts to current error. The main driver.

I - Integral

"The Past". Accumulates error over time. Removes steady-state offset.

D - Derivative

"The Future". Predicts error trend. Adds damping/braking.

Simulation Math

We use a Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4) solver to simulate the physics of the plant. The controller runs a discretized PID equation:

u(t) = Kp*e(t) + Ki*∫e(t)dt + Kd*de(t)/dt

Metrics Guide

  • Overshoot: How much the system exceeds the target. (>10% is usually bad).
  • Rise Time: Time to go from 10% to 90% of target. (Faster is better).
  • Settling Time: Time to stay within 2% of target. (Stability measure).
